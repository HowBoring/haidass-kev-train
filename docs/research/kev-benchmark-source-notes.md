- **Which data:** `decision-v7` trains on 10,000 public records from ten sources, 896 generated legacy-policy records and 1,680 generated compositional records. Its development manifest contains **1,204 records / 1,468 questions**, but its published `clean` scores include **1,264 question rows** after excluding non-clean variants; these are not interchangeable denominators. `transfer-v4` is evaluation-only, comprising six never-trained public sources plus held-out policy/composition structures; development contains **764 records / 764 questions**, but its `clean` scores include **656 question rows**. The transfer manifest declares no trainable sources and no calibration rows. The *test* partitions are separately locked; their manifest counts (decision 1,176 records / 1,440 questions; transfer 764 / 764) must not be used as development denominators or interpreted as clean-test denominators. ## Metric contract and open limits

- For every `clean` question, accuracy is the mean of `argmax(p) == label`. Multiclass Brier is the mean of **sum over all options** `(p_k − one_hot_k)^2` (not binary-only Brier or averaged over options). The benchmark filters `variant == "clean"` before `metrics`, validates option keys/probability sums, and excludes unknowable-source cases from accuracy when present. The `objective` is a **macro average of task NLL**, not the displayed micro clean accuracy/Brier. Published result JSON does contain `clean.nll` and `transfer.clean.nll`; the README/model-card headline tables do **not** report NLL. Do not fabricate NLL from accuracy/Brier, nor conflate reported `objective` with micro NLL. [benchmark](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/90990a5fac2995b9faa3190f7d437e84f2067768/kev/benchmark.py) · [metrics](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/90990a5fac2995b9faa3190f7d437e84f2067768/kev/metrics.py) · [result example](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/90990a5fac2995b9faa3190f7d437e84f2067768/runs/night2-9b-du/00-trial-0/result.json)
- Released Qwen3.5 model-card temperatures: 0.8B **2.41**, 4B **2.14**, 9B **2.30**, fitted on trained-source development rows; `KEV_TEMPERATURE=1.0` restores raw scores. Temperature leaves argmax accuracy unchanged, changes Brier/ECE/NLL. For example, 4B transfer dev raw Brier **0.299** versus as-served **0.264**, and 9B **0.286** versus **0.264**. `KEV_DATE_FACTS=1` is an additional opt-in preprocessor **not included** in headline figures. Published trial `result.json` also includes a `calibrated_clean` calculation with a separately reported trial temperature: do not silently substitute this for model-card as-served values. - `paired_flip` is **pair-level**, not ordinary question accuracy: on complete `a`/`b` pairs in the clean rows, `flip_rate` is the fraction whose predicted option changes when the known label changes; `both_correct_rate` requires **both siblings** to match their own labels. Same-label pairs, if present, have separate invariance measures. The transfer trial reports 64 changing-label pairs (and 24 invariant pairs), while the trained-source trial reports 80 (and 32); neither denominator is all clean questions. Option-order `permutation.flip_rate` is an **entirely different** metric. [contrastive paired_flip](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/90990a5fac2995b9faa3190f7d437e84f2067768/kev/contrastive.py) · [benchmark summarizer](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/90990a5fac2995b9faa3190f7d437e84f2067768/kev/benchmark.py) · [0.6B trial result](https://github.com/jaredpalmer/kev/blob/90990a5fac2995b9faa3190f7d437e84f2067768/runs/v7-06b/02-trial-2/result.json)
- Unknown without additional matched evaluation: whether a local run uses identical clean question rows, class/option ordering, preprocessing, checkpoint/temperature, and split; whether its local NLL follows Kev's exact-from-logits versus floored-probability convention. Manifest count equality alone does not establish question identity. The public cards' approximate three-decimal scores do not establish a statistically significant gap; matched paired rows or confidence intervals would be needed.
